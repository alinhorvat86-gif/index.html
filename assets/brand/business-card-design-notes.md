# ISS Business Card Design — reference template (saved 2026-09-29)

Two-sided card, rounded corners, portrait orientation.

## Side A (front) — "business-card-front.webp"
- Top ~65%: full-bleed London skyline photo at dusk (Tower Bridge, Shard, City skyline, lit up)
- Navy band (~15%): logo (blue/red circuit-shield with keyhole) on right, "INTERNATIONAL SECURITY SYSTEM" in bold white left-aligned, subtitle line "Manned Guarding · CCTV Monitoring · Risk Management", then "SIA-CERTIFIED | LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM" in blue/white below
- Bottom red band (~20%): two white QR code squares side by side, centered

## Side B (back) — "business-card-back.webp"
- Same navy header band at TOP (logo + name + services line + SIA-CERTIFIED line)
- Below: same London skyline dusk photo, full-bleed, no red band/QR codes

## Colors
- Navy: deep blue (~#0d1b3a range)
- Red band: solid red (~#a52129 range)
- Accent blue: light blue for subtitle text (~#5c9bd5 range)
- White text/logo on navy and red

## Reuse instructions
When asked for future print materials (flyers, cards, follow-up orders) in the same brand identity, match this exact layout/color system and reuse the same London dusk skyline photo + navy/red band structure + QR code placement pattern.
